## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the essence of modern, elevated healthcare: clinical rigor merged with human empathy and hospitality-level sophistication. Designed for high-end aesthetic, surgical, and preventative dentistry, the visual tone departs from sterile clinical coldness in favor of a serene, architectural elegance.

The target audience encompasses discerning patients seeking exceptional cosmetic and health outcomes, valuing precision, transparency, calm reassurance, and painless precision. 

The aesthetic is rooted in **Minimalism** enriched with subtle **glassmorphism** overlays and studio-grade editorial layout principles:
- **Atmospheric Clarity:** Crisp, expansive white space grounded by deep teal tones and softened by luminous turquoise accents.
- **Human Connection:** Warm, intimate photographic art direction showing authentic expressions of relief and confidence.
- **Architectural Poise:** Deliberate typographic scale, asymmetric balanced grid alignments, and feather-soft elevation creating a calming, frictionless digital sanctuary.

## Colors

The palette is tuned around restorative mineral and oceanic tones, invoking serenity, clinical precision, and immaculate freshness.

### Roles & Semantic Mapping
- **Primary (`#123C42` - Deep Petroleum Teal):** Used for authoritative brand presence, primary headers, high-impact buttons, and solid interactive focal points.
- **Secondary (`#236A70` - Rich Teal-Cyan):** Employed for supportive actions, focused interactive states, icons, and tinted structural backgrounds.
- **Tertiary (`#49B9C2` - Gentle Turquoise):** An accent of vitality and light. Used for badges, active pills, indicators, subtle glows, and delicate interactive highlights.
- **Backgrounds & Canvas:**
  - `Surface Canvas`: Ice White (`#F7F8F7`) provides a softened, non-glare digital substrate.
  - `Surface Card / Pure`: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) for crisp elevation, clinical cards, and floating floating controls.
  - `Surface Subtle`: Pale Mineral Grey (`#E8EBEA`) for hairline dividers, input frames, and passive badge fills.
- **Typography & Content:**
  - `Text Primary`: Graphite (`#202526`) for high-contrast, effortlessly readable headlines and crucial metadata.
  - `Text Secondary`: Muted Slate (`#737A7B`) for body paragraphs, secondary specs, and captions.
  - `Text Inverted`: `#FFFFFF` on deep teal and rich cyan surfaces.

## Typography

The type system blends the contemporary, geometric poise of **Manrope** with the flawless micro-legibility of **Inter**.

- **Editorial Headlines (Manrope):** Set with snug letter tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) and medium-to-semibold weights (`500` - `600`). This ensures a modern, clean, design-studio cadence that eschews clinical stiffness. Eyebrow headers ("Our Features /", "About Clinic /") pair uppercase `label-sm` with a secondary color tone to create crisp architectural anchors.
- **Functional Body (Inter):** Applied across all reading copy, patient reports, booking funnels, and descriptions. Set with generous line heights to preserve visual breathing space and eliminate eye strain.
- **Micro-labels & Category Tags (Manrope):** High legibility at small sizes with slightly expanded tracking to maintain presence across pills, status cards, and table metadata.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is constructed upon an asymmetrical 12-column fluid grid on desktop (max width `1360px`) and transitions gracefully into a single-column rhythm on mobile devices.

### Form Factors & Responsive Behavior
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters and `3rem` (48px) horizontal margins. Employs wide, gallery-style split compositions (e.g., strong photographic feature paired with offset headline and meta chips).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 columns with `1.25rem` (20px) gutters and `2rem` (32px) margins. Grids collapse into balanced 2-column modules.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4 columns with `1rem` (16px) gutters and `1.25rem` (20px) margins. Horizontal scroll carousels are leveraged for dentist profiles, procedure snapshots, and patient reviews to preserve spatial openness without dense stacking.

### Spacing Cadence
A 4px/8px baseline rhythm guides element spacing:
- Internal card padding favors roomy breathing intervals (`space-lg` and `space-xl`).
- Section breaks on landing pages maintain a substantial rhythm of `4rem` to `6rem` to prevent information overload.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy favors clean planar stacking and atmospheric depth over aggressive drop shadows.

- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Background baseline at `#F7F8F7`.
- **Level 1 (Clinical Cards & Containers):** `#FFFFFF` surfaces bounded by a crisp 1px hairline border in `#E8EBEA`. Shadows are diffuse and cool: `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(18, 60, 66, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Elements & Doctor Cards):** Lifted cards (such as doctor badges layered over hero imagery) utilize a frosted glass composition:
  `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`, and a subtle ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(18, 60, 66, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays, Floating Navbars):** High-clarity frosted panels bounded by pure white hairlines with `box-shadow: 0 20px 48px -8px rgba(18, 60, 66, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a refined geometric curve language with moderate roundedness (`roundedness: 2`).

- Standard cards, interactive inputs, and modal wrappers adhere to `16px` (`rounded-lg`).
- Feature imagery and prominent hero photography adopt `20px` to `24px` (`rounded-xl`), creating soft windowing that balances clinical discipline with organic warmth.
- Badges, status markers, navigation links, and primary CTA buttons use full pill rounding (`9999px`) to maintain approachable tactile targets.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), deep petroleum teal background (`#123C42`), white typography (`#FFFFFF`). Padding: `14px 28px`. Incorporates an accompanying directional arrow icon or trailing pill indicator. Hover transition to `#236A70`.
- **Secondary / Glass Button:** Semi-translucent white surface (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)` over dark imagery, or pure white on light canvas) with an ultrathin 1px border (`#E8EBEA`). Text in `#123C42`.
- **Text Button:** Graphite text (`#202526`) with an adjacent circular arrow icon button (`32px` diameter) for minimal editorial navigation.

### Chips & Tags
- **Procedure Badges:** Pill-shaped with a delicate frosted white fill (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`), text in `#123C42` (13px, weight 500). Hover triggers a subtle turquoise tint (`#49B9C2`) with 10% opacity.
- **Eyebrow Meta Tags:** All-caps Manrope `11px`, letter-spacing `0.08em`, in `#236A70` paired with slash dividers (`/`).

### Cards & Service Tiles
- **Procedure Gallery Card:** Vertical cards with rounded imagery (`16px`), an integrated title overlaid or anchored below, and an arrow disc button in the lower corner for exploration.
- **Doctor Specialist Card:** Horizontal compact card with a circular or soft-square profile photo (`48px` x `48px`), practitioner name in `#202526` (`title-md`), specialty in `#737A7B` (`body-sm`), and a pill rating badge (e.g., "7 year Experience (4.5 Rating)").

### Form Inputs & Controls
- **Text Fields:** White background (`#FFFFFF`), border in `#E8EBEA`, border-radius `12px`, padding `14px 18px`. On focus: subtle `#49B9C2` border transition accompanied by an ambient `0 0 0 3px rgba(73, 185, 194, 0.15)` ring.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Curved square (`6px` radius) or circular. Neutral state framed in `#E8EBEA`. Active selected state filled with `#123C42` featuring a crisp white glyph.

### Statistics & Trust Anchors
- **Metrics Displays:** Oversized Manrope numbers (`40px`, semibold, `#202526`) with uppercase secondary label stacked underneath (`11px`, `#737A7B`, tracking `0.05em`).
- **Patient Avatar Stacks:** Overlapping circular portraits (`36px`) paired with a bold review summary count (`"750+ Reviews"`).
